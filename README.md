## 🧩 Data Story

- **Source:** Fictional data (inspired by public datasets on Kaggle).
- **Structure:** Each row represents a sales transaction, featuring:
  - `Customer Name`, `Product Category`, `Salesperson`
  - `Order Date`, `Region`, `City`, `Ship City`
  - `Quantity`, `Unit Price`, `Revenue`, `Shipping Fee`
- **Business Goal:** Identify performance patterns, key players, high-impact customers, and regional trends to drive smarter sales decisions.

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

- Cleaned and formatted dataset in Excel.
- Standardized date/time fields and categories.
- Added calculated fields (e.g., Total Revenue, Monthly Groupings).
- Organized variables into:
  - **Independent:** Region, Product, Customer, Date
  - **Dependent:** Revenue, Shipping Fee, Quantity

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Analysis

### Key Trends Identified:
- **Seasonality:** High sales in **November**, dips in **February** and **April**.
- **Top Performers:**
  - **Regions:** North dominates overall.
  - **Cities:** Portland and New York consistently lead.
  - **Salespersons:** Nancy Freehafer and Andrew Cecini top charts.
- **Customer Concentration:** Top 10 customers make up a significant revenue chunk.
- **Pricing Patterns:** Most transactions fall under the **₦1,000** mark.

### Tools Used:
- Pivot Tables
- Slicers & Dynamic Filtering
- Multi-chart Dashboards

## 📍 Key Insights

- **Best Product Category:** Beverages dominate sales consistently.
- **Top Revenue Generators:** Nancy (salesperson) and Portland (city).
- **Underperformers:** Dried Fruits & Nuts category; Jan (salesperson).
- **Transactional Volume:** Most transactions occur between ₦0–₦1,000.
- **Repeat Customers:** Company D, H, and J were strong repeat buyers across months and cities.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Product Strategy**
   - Rebrand or remove underperforming categories.
   - Focus inventory on fast-moving items.

2. **Sales Optimization**
   - Offer incentives to top salespeople.
   - Provide coaching or retraining for underperformers.

3. **Regional Growth**
   - Strengthen sales efforts in the West and South.
   - Run targeted promotions in underperforming cities.

4. **Customer Loyalty**
   - Offer loyalty bonuses to top buyers.
   - Create targeted campaigns for inactive customers.

## 🧠 Conclusion

This project highlighted the value of structured data analysis for uncovering hidden trends, enhancing customer targeting, and optimizing sales strategies. While the dataset was fictional, the approach mirrors real-world sales performance projects.
